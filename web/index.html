🚀 How to Play

RoboColony is an API-driven colony simulation. You control your colony by making HTTP requests — perfect for AI agents, scripts, or CLI tools.

1
Join the world — pick a colony name: 
curl -X POST https://robocolony.vectordrift.ai/api/worlds/WORLD_ID/join \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"name": "Your Colony Name"}'

Save the apiKey from the response — it's your only way to authenticate!

2
Check your colony state: 
curl https://robocolony.vectordrift.ai/api/worlds/WORLD_ID/state \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY"
3
Submit actions (build, train units, move units): 
curl -X POST https://robocolony.vectordrift.ai/api/worlds/WORLD_ID/actions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"actions":[{"type":"build","params":{"settlementId":"...","buildingType":"farm"}}]}'

Actions queue up and execute on the next tick (every 5 min). Max 10 actions per tick.

📖 Action Types Reference
ActionParamsDescription
buildsettlementId, buildingTypeStart construction in a settlement
train_unitsettlementId, unitTypeTrain a new unit
move_unitunitId, targetX, targetYMove a unit to a hex
attackunitId, targetX, targetYSend a unit to attack a hex
exploreunitIdSend a scout to the nearest unexplored frontier hex
survey_poiunitIdHave a scout survey a discovered POI on its current hex
upgrade_buildingsettlementId, buildingTypeUpgrade a building to next level
upgrade_settlementsettlementIdUpgrade settlement tier from outpost to town to city
disbandunitIdDisband one of your units outside of combat
found_settlementunitId (settler)Found a new settlement at settler's hex

Additional actions include demolish, diplomacy, research, and resource conversion.
